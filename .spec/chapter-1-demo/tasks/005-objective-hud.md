## Description
Implement the objective HUD as a small autoload service so it survives stage swaps and can never show
a stale stage's text. On `EventBus.stage_changed`, update a dynamic Label with the new objective; show
the HUD when the objective is non-empty and hide it when empty (menu and ending stages). Because the
update happens on `stage_changed` — emitted at fade-start in task 002 — the HUD reflects the new stage
immediately, never the previous one. The Label inherits the CJK theme from task 001 (objectives are
Chinese) and contains no baked values. Follow `design.md`.

## Acceptance
- [ ] `ObjectiveHud` renders the objective from `stage_changed(stage_index, objective)` and updates immediately on every stage change (scope AC #10).

- [ ] `godot --headless --editor --path . --quit-after 10` and `godot --headless --path . --quit-after 5 scenes/boot.tscn` are clean.
