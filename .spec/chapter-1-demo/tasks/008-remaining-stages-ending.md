## Description
Build the remaining three stages and the ending screen, reusing the stage scaffold from task 007.
Field road: a walking corridor ending in a `StageExit` zone (no required NPC). Town: Ina
(`ina.tres`, does not advance — she is the side blue-soda beat) and the wine-shop owner
(`wine_shop.tres`, advances after the buy-wine beat), plus the wine-shop prop. Return: Uma and Defa
(`uma_defa.tres`, the giant-statue-glow trio beat, advances on completion) with the wine-cart prop.
Ending: a Control scene showing "Chapter 1 DEMO complete" that, after a short delay, calls
`GameFlow.request_quit_to_menu()` so the demo auto-returns to the menu (the ending stage is tagged
`&"chapter"` in FlowConfig, so `request_quit_to_menu` is valid there). Register every new scene. Follow
`design.md`.
